# Bank Customer Churn Analysis | Data Analytics Project

**Understanding Customer Behaviour. Identifying Churn Patterns. Driving Better Business Decisions.**

Customer retention is one of the biggest challenges in the banking industry. Understanding why customers leave and identifying the factors that influence their decisions can help financial institutions build stronger customer relationships and improve long-term business performance.

This project focuses on analyzing bank customer churn to uncover meaningful patterns, explore customer behaviour, and identify the key factors contributing to customer attrition. By transforming raw customer data into meaningful insights, the project aims to demonstrate how data analytics can help businesses make smarter, evidence-based decisions.

**Key Areas of Analysis:**

- **Customer Demographics:** Analyze customer profiles based on age, gender, geography, and other demographic attributes.
- **Customer Churn Analysis:** Identify the number and percentage of customers who have exited the bank.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Explore customer groups based on their characteristics, account activity, and financial behaviour.
- **Account & Financial Insights:** Examine credit scores, account balances, estimated salaries, product ownership, and customer activity.
- **Churn Patterns & Trends:** Identify relationships between customer attributes and churn behaviour.
- **Customer Retention Opportunities:** Discover potential areas where banks can improve customer satisfaction and reduce customer attrition.

**Project Objectives:**

1. Understand the overall customer churn rate.
2. Identify customer segments with higher churn tendencies.
3. Explore the relationship between customer characteristics and attrition.
4. Extract actionable insights that can support customer retention strategies.
5. Demonstrate the practical application of data analytics in the banking and financial services industry.

**Tools & Skills Demonstrated:**
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Data Preparation
- Data Analysis
- Data Modeling
- Pivot Tables and KPI Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence and Analytical Thinking

**Business Impact:**

The ultimate goal of this project is to demonstrate how data can go beyond numbers and become a valuable business asset. By identifying churn patterns and understanding customer behaviour, banks can develop targeted retention strategies, improve customer engagement, strengthen customer loyalty, and make more informed business decisions.
